S6 SURVEY-METHOD MEASUREMENT INVARIANCE — FINAL VALIDATION RECORD

The exact historical S6 .inp files were not recovered. The final syntax in this package
was reconstructed from the recovered Mplus workflow and documented measurement model,
then rerun with the final long-format samples in Mplus 9.1. The rerun reproduces every
fit statistic currently reported in Supplementary Table S6.

Specification recovered/validated:
- two survey-method groups: online and face-to-face;
- CLUSTER = ID and TYPE = COMPLEX;
- 15 PA/NA/travel-satisfaction indicators centred within participant using
  CENTER ... (GROUPMEAN);
- PA, NA and travel satisfaction modelled as latent factors;
- retained cross-loadings AN BY AP2 and AP BY AN2;
- indicator intercepts and latent means fixed at zero after within-participant centring;
- flexible metric model constrains the 12 primary non-reference loadings equal across
  survey methods, while the two retained cross-loadings remain free.
